Try It 2.19

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. According to the National Automobile Dealers Association, the average cost of a car in 2014 was $\$28{,}400$. This was $\$1{,}600$ less than six times the cost in 1975. What was the average cost of a car in 1975?
  2. US Census data shows that the median price of new home in the U.S. in November 2014 was $\$280{,}900$. This was $\$10{,}700$ more than 14 times the price in November 1964. What was the median price of a new home in November 1964?
Show a hint
Name the unknown amount with a variable. "Less than six times" it is six times it, minus that amount.
Show the answers

  1. The average cost was $\$5{,}000$.

  2. The median price was $\$19{,}300$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Let $c$ = the average cost in 1975. Six times that, less $1{,}600$, is $6c - 1{,}600$.
$28{,}400$$=$$6c - 1{,}600$Translate: the cost in 2014 is $1{,}600$ less than six times the cost in 1975.
$28{,}400 \hl{{} + 1{,}600}$$=$$6c - 1{,}600 \hl{{} + 1{,}600}$To undo subtracting $1{,}600$, add $1{,}600$ to both sides.
$30{,}000$$=$$6c$Simplify.
$\dfrac{30{,}000}{\hl{6}}$$=$$\dfrac{6c}{\hl{6}}$Divide both sides by $6$.
$5{,}000$$=$$c$Simplify.
The average cost of a car in 1975 was $\$5{,}000$.

Check: $6(5{,}000) - 1{,}600 = 30{,}000 - 1{,}600 = 28{,}400$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Let $p$ = the median price in November 1964. Fourteen times that, plus $10{,}700$, is $14p + 10{,}700$.
$280{,}900$$=$$14p + 10{,}700$Translate: the 2014 price is $10{,}700$ more than $14$ times the 1964 price.
$280{,}900 \hl{{} - 10{,}700}$$=$$14p + 10{,}700 \hl{{} - 10{,}700}$To undo adding $10{,}700$, subtract $10{,}700$ from both sides.
$270{,}200$$=$$14p$Simplify.
$\dfrac{270{,}200}{\hl{14}}$$=$$\dfrac{14p}{\hl{14}}$Divide both sides by $14$.
$19{,}300$$=$$p$Simplify.
The median price of a new home in November 1964 was $\$19{,}300$.

Check: $14(19{,}300) + 10{,}700 = 270{,}200 + 10{,}700 = 280{,}900$.
