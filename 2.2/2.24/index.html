Find a Markup and a List Price

Try It 2.24

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Find ⓐ the amount of mark-up and ⓑ the list price: Jim’s music store bought a guitar at original cost $\$1{,}200$. Jim marked the price up $50\%$.
  2. Find ⓐ the amount of mark-up and ⓑ the list price: The Auto Resale Store bought Pablo’s Toyota for $\$8{,}500$. They marked the price up $35\%$.
Show a hint
The mark-up is the percent (as a decimal) times the original cost; the list price is the original cost plus the mark-up.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $\$600$ ⓑ $\$1{,}800$

  2. ⓐ $\$2{,}975$ ⓑ $\$11{,}475$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

ⓐ The amount of mark-up
Let $m$ = the amount of mark-up. The mark-up is $50\%$ of the $\$1{,}200$ original cost.
$m$$=$$0.50 \cdot 1{,}200$Translate: $50\%$ is $0.50$, and "of" means times.
$m$$=$$600$Multiply.
The mark-up on the guitar was $\$600$.
ⓑ The list price
Let $p$ = the list price. The list price is the original cost plus the mark-up.
$p$$=$$1{,}200 + 600$Translate, with the mark-up from part ⓐ.
$p$$=$$1{,}800$Add.
The list price of the guitar was $\$1{,}800$.

Check: the list price is more than the original cost.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

ⓐ The amount of mark-up
Let $m$ = the amount of mark-up. The mark-up is $35\%$ of the $\$8{,}500$ original cost.
$m$$=$$0.35 \cdot 8{,}500$Translate: $35\%$ is $0.35$, and "of" means times.
$m$$=$$2{,}975$Multiply.
The mark-up on the Toyota was $\$2{,}975$.
ⓑ The list price
Let $p$ = the list price. The list price is the original cost plus the mark-up.
$p$$=$$8{,}500 + 2{,}975$Translate, with the mark-up from part ⓐ.
$p$$=$$11{,}475$Add.
The list price of the Toyota was $\$11{,}475$.

Check: the list price is more than the original cost.
