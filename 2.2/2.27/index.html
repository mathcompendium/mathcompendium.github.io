Find the Principal of a Loan

Previous example · Example 2.26Find a Simple Interest Rate

Try It 2.27

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Eduardo noticed that his new car loan papers stated that with a $7.5\%$ simple interest rate, he would pay $\$6{,}596.25$ in interest over five years. How much did he borrow to pay for his car?
  2. In five years, Gloria’s bank account earned $\$2{,}400$ interest at $5\%$ simple interest. How much had she deposited in the account?
Show a hint
The principal is the unknown. Substitute $I$, $r$ and $t$ into $I = Prt$, multiply the numbers, then divide.
Show the answers

  1. He borrowed $\$17{,}590$.

  2. She deposited $\$9{,}600$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Organize the facts: $I = 6{,}596.25$, $r = 7.5\% = 0.075$, $t = 5$. The amount borrowed is the principal. Let $P$ = the principal.
$I$$=$$Prt$Write the simple interest formula.
$6{,}596.25$$=$$P \cdot 0.075 \cdot 5$Substitute $I$, $r$ and $t$.
$6{,}596.25$$=$$0.375P$Multiply the numbers on the right: $0.075 \cdot 5 = 0.375$.
$\dfrac{6{,}596.25}{\hl{0.375}}$$=$$\dfrac{0.375P}{\hl{0.375}}$Divide both sides by $0.375$.
$17{,}590$$=$$P$Simplify.
Eduardo borrowed $\$17{,}590$.

Check: $17{,}590 \cdot 0.075 = 1{,}319.25$, and $1{,}319.25 \cdot 5 = 6{,}596.25$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Organize the facts: $I = 2{,}400$, $r = 5\% = 0.05$, $t = 5$. The amount deposited is the principal. Let $P$ = the principal.
$I$$=$$Prt$Write the simple interest formula.
$2{,}400$$=$$P \cdot 0.05 \cdot 5$Substitute $I$, $r$ and $t$.
$2{,}400$$=$$0.25P$Multiply the numbers on the right: $0.05 \cdot 5 = 0.25$.
$\dfrac{2{,}400}{\hl{0.25}}$$=$$\dfrac{0.25P}{\hl{0.25}}$Divide both sides by $0.25$.
$9{,}600$$=$$P$Simplify.
Gloria had deposited $\$9{,}600$.

Check: $9{,}600 \cdot 0.05 = 480$, and $480 \cdot 5 = 2{,}400$.
