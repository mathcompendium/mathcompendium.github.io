Try It 2.25

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Nathaly deposited $\$12{,}500$ in her bank account where it will earn $4\%$ simple interest. How much interest will Nathaly earn in five years?
  2. Susana invested a principal of $\$36{,}000$ in her bank account that earned simple interest at an interest rate of $6.5\%$. How much interest did she earn in three years?
Show a hint
List $I$, $P$, $r$ and $t$, write the rate as a decimal, and substitute into $I = Prt$.
Show the answers

  1. She will earn $\$2{,}500$.

  2. She earned $\$7{,}020$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Organize the facts: $P = 12{,}500$, $r = 4\% = 0.04$, $t = 5$. Let $I$ = the interest.
$I$$=$$Prt$Write the simple interest formula.
$I$$=$$12{,}500 \cdot 0.04 \cdot 5$Substitute $P$, $r$ and $t$.
$I$$=$$500 \cdot 5$Multiply from left to right: $12{,}500 \cdot 0.04 = 500$, the interest for one year.
$I$$=$$2{,}500$Multiply.
Nathaly will earn $\$2{,}500$ in interest.

Check: $4\%$ of $12{,}500$ is $500$ a year, and over five years that is $2{,}500$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Organize the facts: $P = 36{,}000$, $r = 6.5\% = 0.065$, $t = 3$. Let $I$ = the interest.
$I$$=$$Prt$Write the simple interest formula.
$I$$=$$36{,}000 \cdot 0.065 \cdot 3$Substitute $P$, $r$ and $t$.
$I$$=$$2{,}340 \cdot 3$Multiply from left to right: $36{,}000 \cdot 0.065 = 2{,}340$, the interest for one year.
$I$$=$$7{,}020$Multiply.
Susana earned $\$7{,}020$ in interest.

Check: $6.5\%$ is a little less than $7\%$; $7\%$ of $36{,}000$ is $2{,}520$ a year, $7{,}560$ over three years. $7{,}020$ is a little less.
