Problem 1

ⓐ What number is $45\%$ of $80$?
$n$$=$$0.45 \cdot 80$"What number" is $n$, "is" is $=$, $45\%$ is $0.45$, and "of" means times.
$n$$=$$36$Multiply.
ⓑ $7.5\%$ of what amount is $\$1.95$?
$0.075n$$=$$1.95$$7.5\%$ is $0.075$, "of" means times, and "what amount" is $n$.
$\dfrac{0.075n}{\hl{0.075}}$$=$$\dfrac{1.95}{\hl{0.075}}$Divide both sides by $0.075$.
$n$$=$$26$Simplify. The amount is $\$26$.
ⓒ $110$ is what percent of $88$?
$110$$=$$88p$"Is" is $=$, "what percent" is $p$, and "of $88$" means times $88$.
$\dfrac{110}{\hl{88}}$$=$$\dfrac{88p}{\hl{88}}$Divide both sides by $88$.
$1.25$$=$$p$Simplify.
Write $1.25$ as a percent: move the point two places right. $p = 125\%$.
