Try It 2.23

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Find the percent change. (Round to the nearest tenth of a percent.) In 2011, the IRS increased the deductible mileage cost to $55.5$ cents from $51$ cents.
  2. Find the percent change. (Round to the nearest tenth of a percent.) In 1995, the standard bus fare in Chicago was $\$1.50$. In 2008, the standard bus fare was $\$2.25$.
Show a hint
Find the change first (new minus original), then ask "The change is what percent of the original amount?"
Show the answers

  1. $8.8\%$

  2. $50\%$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Find the amount of change, the new amount minus the original amount: $55.5 - 51 = 4.5$ cents.
The change is what percent of the original amount? Let $p$ = the percent. $4.5$ is what percent of $51$?
$4.5$$=$$p \cdot 51$Translate: "is" is $=$, "what percent" is $p$, and "of" means times.
$4.5$$=$$51p$Write the number first.
$\dfrac{4.5}{\hl{51}}$$=$$\dfrac{51p}{\hl{51}}$Divide both sides by $51$.
$4.5 \div 51 = 0.08823\ldots$ To round the percent to the nearest tenth, round the decimal to the nearest thousandth: $p \approx 0.088$.
Write it as a percent: move the point two places right. $p \approx 8.8\%$.
The mileage cost increased by about $8.8\%$.

Check: $10\%$ of $51$ is $5.1$. The change, $4.5$, is a little less, so the percent should be a little less than $10\%$, and it is.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Find the amount of change, the new amount minus the original amount: $2.25 - 1.50 = 0.75$, or $\$0.75$.
The change is what percent of the original amount? Let $p$ = the percent. $0.75$ is what percent of $1.50$?
$0.75$$=$$p \cdot 1.50$Translate: "is" is $=$, "what percent" is $p$, and "of" means times.
$0.75$$=$$1.50p$Write the number first.
$\dfrac{0.75}{\hl{1.50}}$$=$$\dfrac{1.50p}{\hl{1.50}}$Divide both sides by $1.50$.
$0.5$$=$$p$Simplify.
Write it as a percent: move the point two places right. $p = 50\%$.
The bus fare increased by $50\%$.

Check: $50\%$ of $\$1.50$ is $\$0.75$, the change.
