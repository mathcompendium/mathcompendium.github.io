Try It 2.17

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Find three consecutive integers whose sum is $-96$.
  2. Find three consecutive integers whose sum is $-36$.
Show a hint
Consecutive integers are $n$, $n + 1$, and $n + 2$. Add them and set the sum equal to the given number.
Show the answers

  1. $-33$, $-32$, $-31$

  2. $-13$, $-12$, $-11$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Let $n$ = the first integer. Then $n + 1$ is the second and $n + 2$ is the third.
$n + n + 1 + n + 2$$=$$-96$Translate: the sum of the three integers is $-96$.
$3n + 3$$=$$-96$Combine like terms.
$3n + 3 \hl{{} - 3}$$=$$-96 \hl{{} - 3}$To undo adding $3$, subtract $3$ from both sides.
$3n$$=$$-99$Simplify.
$\dfrac{3n}{\hl{3}}$$=$$\dfrac{-99}{\hl{3}}$Divide both sides by $3$.
$n$$=$$-33$Simplify. This is the first integer.
The second is $n + 1 = -32$ and the third is $n + 2 = -31$.
The three consecutive integers are $-33$, $-32$, and $-31$.

Check: they are consecutive, and $-33 + (-32) + (-31) = -96$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Let $n$ = the first integer. Then $n + 1$ is the second and $n + 2$ is the third.
$n + n + 1 + n + 2$$=$$-36$Translate: the sum of the three integers is $-36$.
$3n + 3$$=$$-36$Combine like terms.
$3n + 3 \hl{{} - 3}$$=$$-36 \hl{{} - 3}$To undo adding $3$, subtract $3$ from both sides.
$3n$$=$$-39$Simplify.
$\dfrac{3n}{\hl{3}}$$=$$\dfrac{-39}{\hl{3}}$Divide both sides by $3$.
$n$$=$$-13$Simplify. This is the first integer.
The second is $n + 1 = -12$ and the third is $n + 2 = -11$.
The three consecutive integers are $-13$, $-12$, and $-11$.

Check: they are consecutive, and $-13 + (-12) + (-11) = -36$.
