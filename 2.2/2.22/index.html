Find the Percent of Calories from Fat

Try It 2.22

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Mitzi received some gourmet brownies as a gift. The wrapper said each brownie was $480$ calories, and had $240$ calories of fat. What percent of the total calories in each brownie comes from fat? Round the answer to the nearest whole percent.
  2. The mix Ricardo plans to use to make brownies says that each brownie will be $190$ calories, and $76$ calories are from fat. What percent of the total calories are from fat? Round the answer to the nearest whole percent.
Show a hint
Ask "What percent of the total is the part?", translate it, solve for $p$, and write the decimal as a percent.
Show the answers

  1. $50\%$

  2. $40\%$

Problem 1

Let $p$ = the percent of the calories from fat. The question: what percent of $480$ is $240$?
$p \cdot 480$$=$$240$Translate: "what percent" is $p$, "of" means times, and "is" is $=$.
$480p$$=$$240$Write the number first.
$\dfrac{480p}{\hl{480}}$$=$$\dfrac{240}{\hl{480}}$Divide both sides by $480$.
$p$$=$$0.5$Simplify.
Write $0.5$ as a percent: move the point two places right. $p = 50\%$.

Check: $50\%$ is one half, and $240$ is one half of $480$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Let $p$ = the percent of the calories from fat. The question: what percent of $190$ is $76$?
$p \cdot 190$$=$$76$Translate: "what percent" is $p$, "of" means times, and "is" is $=$.
$190p$$=$$76$Write the number first.
$\dfrac{190p}{\hl{190}}$$=$$\dfrac{76}{\hl{190}}$Divide both sides by $190$.
$p$$=$$0.4$Simplify.
Write $0.4$ as a percent: move the point two places right. $p = 40\%$.

Check: $0.40(190) = 76$.
