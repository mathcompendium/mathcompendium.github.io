Find Three Consecutive Even Integers

Try It 2.18

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Find three consecutive even integers whose sum is $102$.
  2. Find three consecutive even integers whose sum is $-24$.
Show a hint
Consecutive even integers are two apart: $n$, $n + 2$, and $n + 4$.
Show the answers

  1. $32$, $34$, $36$

  2. $-10$, $-8$, $-6$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Let $n$ = the first even integer. Then $n + 2$ is the second and $n + 4$ is the third.
$n + n + 2 + n + 4$$=$$102$Translate: the sum of the three even integers is $102$.
$3n + 6$$=$$102$Combine like terms.
$3n + 6 \hl{{} - 6}$$=$$102 \hl{{} - 6}$To undo adding $6$, subtract $6$ from both sides.
$3n$$=$$96$Simplify.
$\dfrac{3n}{\hl{3}}$$=$$\dfrac{96}{\hl{3}}$Divide both sides by $3$.
$n$$=$$32$Simplify. This is the first even integer.
The second is $n + 2 = 34$ and the third is $n + 4 = 36$.
The three consecutive even integers are $32$, $34$, and $36$.

Check: they are consecutive even integers, and $32 + 34 + 36 = 102$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Let $n$ = the first even integer. Then $n + 2$ is the second and $n + 4$ is the third.
$n + n + 2 + n + 4$$=$$-24$Translate: the sum of the three even integers is $-24$.
$3n + 6$$=$$-24$Combine like terms.
$3n + 6 \hl{{} - 6}$$=$$-24 \hl{{} - 6}$To undo adding $6$, subtract $6$ from both sides.
$3n$$=$$-30$Simplify.
$\dfrac{3n}{\hl{3}}$$=$$\dfrac{-30}{\hl{3}}$Divide both sides by $3$.
$n$$=$$-10$Simplify. This is the first even integer.
The second is $n + 2 = -8$ and the third is $n + 4 = -6$.
The three consecutive even integers are $-10$, $-8$, and $-6$.

Check: they are consecutive even integers, and $-10 + (-8) + (-6) = -24$.
