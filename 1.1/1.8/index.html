Try It 1.8

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Translate into an algebraic expression: ⓐ the difference of $14x^2$ and $13$ ⓑ the quotient of $12x$ and $2$ ⓒ $13$ more than $z$ ⓓ $18$ less than $8x$
  2. Translate into an algebraic expression: ⓐ the sum of $17y^2$ and $19$ ⓑ the product of $7$ and $y$ ⓒ eleven more than $x$ ⓓ fourteen less than $11a$
Show a hint
"The difference of $a$ and $b$" keeps the order: $a - b$. "$b$ less than $a$" reverses it: $18$ less than $8x$ starts with $8x$.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $14x^2 - 13$ ⓑ $12x \div 2$ ⓒ $z + 13$ ⓓ $8x - 18$

  2. ⓐ $17y^2 + 19$ ⓑ $7y$ ⓒ $x + 11$ ⓓ $11a - 14$

These are Try Its 1.15 and 1.16 in Intermediate Algebra 2e, Section 1.1 (OpenStax, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The hint is ours.
