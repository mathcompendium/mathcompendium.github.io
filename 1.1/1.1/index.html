Try It 1.1

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Is $4{,}962$ divisible byⓐ $2$?ⓑ $3$?ⓒ $5$?ⓓ $6$?ⓔ $10$?
  2. Is $3{,}765$ divisible byⓐ $2$?ⓑ $3$?ⓒ $5$?ⓓ $6$?ⓔ $10$?
Show a hint
Use the tests: $2$ needs an even last digit, $3$ needs a digit sum divisible by $3$, $5$ needs a last digit of $0$ or $5$, $6$ needs both $2$ and $3$, and $10$ needs a last digit of $0$.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ yesⓑ yesⓒ noⓓ yesⓔ no

    The last digit, $2$, is even, and the digits add to $4 + 9 + 6 + 2 = 21$, which is divisible by $3$.

  2. ⓐ noⓑ yesⓒ yesⓓ noⓔ no

    The last digit, $5$, is odd, and the digits add to $3 + 7 + 6 + 5 = 21$, which is divisible by $3$.
