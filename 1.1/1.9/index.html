Try It 1.9

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Translate into an algebraic expression: ⓐ four times the sum of $p$ and $q$ ⓑ the sum of four times $p$ and $q$
  2. Translate into an algebraic expression: ⓐ the difference of two times $x$ and $8$ ⓑ two times the difference of $x$ and $8$
Show a hint
Find the two operation words. When the whole sum or difference is multiplied, put it in parentheses.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $4(p + q)$ ⓑ $4p + q$

  2. ⓐ $2x - 8$ ⓑ $2(x - 8)$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

ⓐ four times the sum of $p$ and $q$
$4(p + q)$"Four times" multiplies the whole sum, so the sum goes in parentheses.
ⓑ the sum of four times $p$ and $q$
$4p + q$This is a sum of two things, $4p$ and $q$, so no parentheses are needed.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

ⓐ the difference of two times $x$ and $8$
$2x - 8$This is a difference of two things, $2x$ and $8$, so no parentheses are needed.
ⓑ two times the difference of $x$ and $8$
$2(x - 8)$"Two times" multiplies the whole difference, so the difference goes in parentheses.
