Try It 1.32

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Simplify: $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{3}}$.
  2. Simplify: $\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{3}}$.
Show a hint
Combine the fractions on top and on the bottom, each with its own LCD. Then divide the top by the bottom.
Show the answers

  1. $2$

    $\dfrac{5}{6} \div \dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{5}{6} \cdot \dfrac{12}{5} = 2$.

  2. $\dfrac{2}{7}$

    $\dfrac{1}{6} \div \dfrac{7}{12} = \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{12}{7} = \dfrac{2}{7}$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{3}}$The problem. Simplify the top and the bottom separately.
$=$$\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{6} + \dfrac{3}{6}}{\dfrac{9}{12} - \dfrac{4}{12}}$The LCD on the top is $6$; on the bottom it is $12$.
$=$$\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{6}}{\dfrac{5}{12}}$Add on the top and subtract on the bottom.
$=$$\dfrac{5}{6} \cdot \dfrac{12}{5}$Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the bottom.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{5} \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{6}}{\cancel{6} \cdot \cancel{5}}$$12 = 2 \cdot 6$. Divide out the $5$ and the $6$.
$=$$2$
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{3} - \dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{3}}$The problem. Simplify the top and the bottom separately.
$=$$\dfrac{\dfrac{4}{6} - \dfrac{3}{6}}{\dfrac{3}{12} + \dfrac{4}{12}}$The LCD on the top is $6$; on the bottom it is $12$.
$=$$\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{6}}{\dfrac{7}{12}}$Subtract on the top and add on the bottom.
$=$$\dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{12}{7}$Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the bottom.
$=$$\dfrac{1 \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{6}}{\cancel{6} \cdot 7}$$12 = 2 \cdot 6$. Divide out the $6$.
$=$$\dfrac{2}{7}$
