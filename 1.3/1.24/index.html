Try It 1.24

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Simplify: $-\dfrac{69}{120}$.
  2. Simplify: $-\dfrac{120}{192}$.
Show a hint
Write the numerator and denominator as products of primes, then divide out the common factors.
Show the answers

  1. $-\dfrac{23}{40}$

    $69 = 3 \cdot 23$ and $120 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$; divide out the common $3$.

  2. $-\dfrac{5}{8}$

    $120 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5$ and $192 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$; divide out $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$-\dfrac{69}{120}$The problem.
$=$$-\dfrac{3 \cdot 23}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5}$Write the numerator and the denominator as products of primes. The negative sign stays in front.
$=$$-\dfrac{\cancel{3} \cdot 23}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{3} \cdot 5}$Divide out the common factor, $3$.
$=$$-\dfrac{23}{40}$Multiply what is left: $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 = 40$.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$-\dfrac{120}{192}$The problem.
$=$$-\dfrac{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}$Write each as a product of primes.
$=$$-\dfrac{\cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{3} \cdot 5}{\cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{3}}$Divide out the common factors: three $2$s and a $3$.
$=$$-\dfrac{5}{8}$What is left: $5$ over $2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8$.
