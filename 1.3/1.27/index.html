Simplify a Complex Fraction with Variables

Try It 1.27

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Simplify: $\dfrac{\dfrac{a}{8}}{\dfrac{ab}{6}}$.
  2. Simplify: $\dfrac{\dfrac{p}{2}}{\dfrac{pq}{8}}$.
Show a hint
Rewrite the complex fraction as division, multiply by the reciprocal, then divide out common factors.
Show the answers

  1. $\dfrac{3}{4b}$

    $\dfrac{a}{8} \div \dfrac{ab}{6} = \dfrac{a}{8} \cdot \dfrac{6}{ab} = \dfrac{6}{8b} = \dfrac{3}{4b}$.

  2. $\dfrac{4}{q}$

    $\dfrac{p}{2} \cdot \dfrac{8}{pq} = \dfrac{8}{2q} = \dfrac{4}{q}$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$\dfrac{\dfrac{a}{8}}{\dfrac{ab}{6}}$The problem. The main fraction bar means divide.
$=$$\dfrac{a}{8} \div \dfrac{ab}{6}$Rewrite it as division.
$=$$\dfrac{a}{8} \cdot \dfrac{6}{ab}$Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
$=$$\dfrac{a \cdot 6}{8 \cdot ab}$Multiply the numerators and the denominators.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{a} \cdot 3 \cdot \cancel{2}}{4 \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{a} \cdot b}$$6 = 3 \cdot 2$ and $8 = 4 \cdot 2$. Divide out the $a$ and the $2$.
$=$$\dfrac{3}{4b}$
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\dfrac{\dfrac{p}{2}}{\dfrac{pq}{8}}$The problem. The main fraction bar means divide.
$=$$\dfrac{p}{2} \div \dfrac{pq}{8}$Rewrite it as division.
$=$$\dfrac{p}{2} \cdot \dfrac{8}{pq}$Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
$=$$\dfrac{p \cdot 8}{2 \cdot pq}$Multiply the numerators and the denominators.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{p} \cdot 4 \cdot \cancel{2}}{\cancel{2} \cdot \cancel{p} \cdot q}$$8 = 4 \cdot 2$. Divide out the $p$ and the $2$.
$=$$\dfrac{4}{q}$
