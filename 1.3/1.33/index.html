Try It 1.33

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Evaluate $3ab^2$ when $a = -\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $b = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. Evaluate $4c^3d$ when $c = -\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $d = -\dfrac{4}{3}$.
Show a hint
Substitute each fraction in parentheses. Do the exponent first, then multiply and divide out common factors.
Show the answers

  1. $-\dfrac{1}{2}$

    $3\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 = 3\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

  2. $\dfrac{2}{3}$

    $4\left(-\dfrac{1}{8}\right)\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\right) = \dfrac{16}{24} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$3\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2$Replace $a$ and $b$, each in its own parentheses. Only $b$ is squared.
$=$$3\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)$Exponent first: $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}$, since a negative times a negative is positive.
$=$$-\dfrac{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1}{3 \cdot 4}$One negative factor, so the product is negative. Multiply the numerators and the denominators.
$=$$-\dfrac{\cancel{3} \cdot \cancel{2}}{\cancel{3} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot 2}$$4 = 2 \cdot 2$. Divide out the $3$ and a $2$.
$=$$-\dfrac{1}{2}$
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$4\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\right)$Replace $c$ and $d$, each in its own parentheses. Only $c$ is cubed.
$=$$4\left(-\dfrac{1}{8}\right)\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\right)$Exponent first: three negative factors make a negative, so $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = -\frac{1}{8}$.
$=$$\dfrac{4 \cdot 1 \cdot 4}{8 \cdot 3}$Two negative factors, so the product is positive. Multiply the numerators and the denominators.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{4} \cdot 2 \cdot \cancel{2}}{\cancel{4} \cdot \cancel{2} \cdot 3}$$8 = 4 \cdot 2$, and the other $4 = 2 \cdot 2$. Divide out the $4$ and a $2$.
$=$$\dfrac{2}{3}$
