Try It 1.12

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Fill in $<$, $>$, or $=$: ⓐ $-9 \ \underline{\quad}\ -|-9|$ ⓑ $2 \ \underline{\quad}\ -|-2|$ ⓒ $-8 \ \underline{\quad}\ |-8|$ ⓓ $-(-9) \ \underline{\quad}\ |-9|$
  2. Fill in $<$, $>$, or $=$: ⓐ $7 \ \underline{\quad}\ -|-7|$ ⓑ $-(-10) \ \underline{\quad}\ |-10|$ ⓒ $|-4| \ \underline{\quad}\ -|-4|$ ⓓ $-1 \ \underline{\quad}\ |-1|$
Show a hint
Simplify each side first. An absolute value is never negative, and a minus sign outside the bars is applied after.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $=$ ⓑ $>$ ⓒ $<$ ⓓ $=$

    Simplified: ⓐ $-9$ and $-9$ ⓑ $2$ and $-2$ ⓒ $-8$ and $8$ ⓓ $9$ and $9$.

  2. ⓐ $>$ ⓑ $=$ ⓒ $>$ ⓓ $<$

    Simplified: ⓐ $7$ and $-7$ ⓑ $10$ and $10$ ⓒ $4$ and $-4$ ⓓ $-1$ and $1$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$-9$$=$$-|-9|$ⓐ $|-9| = 9$, and the negative sign in front makes it $-9$. Both sides are $-9$.
$2$$>$$-|-2|$ⓑ The right side is $-|-2| = -2$. $2$ is to the right of $-2$ on the number line, so it is greater.
$-8$$<$$|-8|$ⓒ $|-8| = 8$. $-8$ is to the left of $8$, so it is less.
$-(-9)$$=$$|-9|$ⓓ The opposite of $-9$ is $9$, and $|-9| = 9$. Both sides are $9$.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$7$$>$$-|-7|$ⓐ $-|-7| = -7$, and $7$ is to the right of $-7$.
$-(-10)$$=$$|-10|$ⓑ The opposite of $-10$ is $10$, and $|-10| = 10$.
$|-4|$$>$$-|-4|$ⓒ The left side is $4$ and the right side is $-4$. Careful: the negative sign is applied after the absolute value.
$-1$$<$$|-1|$ⓓ $|-1| = 1$, and $-1$ is to the left of $1$.
