  1. Simplify: $8 - (-3 - 1) - 9$.

Show a hint
Simplify inside the parentheses first. Subtracting a negative number is adding its opposite.
Show the answers

  1. $3$

    $8 - (-4) - 9 = 8 + 4 - 9 = 3$.

  2. $13$

    $12 - (-15) - 14 = 12 + 15 - 14 = 13$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$8 - (-3 - 1) - 9$The problem. Parentheses first.
$=$$8 - (-4) - 9$$-3 - 1 = -4$.
$=$$8 + 4 - 9$Subtracting $-4$ is the same as adding $4$.
$=$$12 - 9$Work from left to right: $8 + 4 = 12$.
$=$$3$
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$12 - (-9 - 6) - 14$The problem. Parentheses first.
$=$$12 - (-15) - 14$$-9 - 6 = -15$.
$=$$12 + 15 - 14$Subtracting $-15$ is the same as adding $15$. Careful: it is not $12 - 15$.
$=$$27 - 14$Work from left to right: $12 + 15 = 27$.
$=$$13$
