Try It 1.23

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. In the morning, the temperature in Anchorage, Alaska was $15$ degrees. By mid-afternoon the temperature had dropped to $30$ degrees below zero. What was the difference in the morning and afternoon temperatures?
  2. The temperature in Denver was $-6$ degrees at lunchtime. By sunset the temperature had dropped to $-15$ degrees. What was the difference in the lunchtime and sunset temperatures?
Show a hint
"The difference of $a$ and $b$" is $a - b$. "Below zero" is a negative number.
Show the answers

  1. The difference in temperatures was $45$ degrees.

    $15 - (-30) = 15 + 30 = 45$.

  2. The difference in temperatures was $9$ degrees.

    $-6 - (-15) = -6 + 15 = 9$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

The two temperatures are $15$ degrees and $-30$ degrees ($30$ below zero).
$15 - (-30)$"The difference of $15$ and $-30$": subtract, in the order given.
$=$$15 + 30$Subtracting $-30$ is the same as adding $30$.
$=$$45$Add.
Answer with a sentence: the difference in temperatures was $45$ degrees.
Problem 2

$=$$-6 + 15$Subtracting $-15$ is the same as adding $15$.
$=$$9$Add.
