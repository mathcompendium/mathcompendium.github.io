The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

ⓐ $(-3)^4$
$(-3)^4$The base is $-3$, because of the parentheses.
$=$$(-3)(-3)(-3)(-3)$So $-3$ is a factor four times.
$=$$81$$(-3)(-3) = 9$, $9(-3) = -27$, and $-27(-3) = 81$.
ⓑ $-3^4$
$-3^4$Without parentheses, the base is $3$, and the negative is applied after the power.
$=$$-(3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3)$So $3$ is a factor four times.
$=$$-81$$3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = 81$, and the negative sign makes it $-81$.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

ⓐ $(-7)^2$
$(-7)^2$The base is $-7$.
$=$$(-7)(-7)$$-7$ is a factor two times.
$=$$49$A negative times a negative is positive.
ⓑ $-7^2$
$-7^2$The base is $7$, and the negative is applied after the power.
$=$$-(7 \cdot 7)$
$=$$-49$Careful: the parentheses make the difference, $49$ with them and $-49$ without.
