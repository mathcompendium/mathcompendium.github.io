  2. $x = -2$

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$\dfrac{1}{4}x + \dfrac{1}{2}$$=$$\dfrac{5}{8}$The problem. The denominators are $4$, $2$ and $8$, so the LCD is $8$.
$\hl{8}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}x + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$$=$$\hl{8}\left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)$Multiply both sides by the LCD, $8$.
$2x + 4$$=$$5$Distribute: $8 \cdot \frac{1}{4}x = 2x$, $8 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 4$ and $8 \cdot \frac{5}{8} = 5$. No more fractions.
$2x + 4 \hl{{} - 4}$$=$$5 \hl{{} - 4}$To undo adding $4$, subtract $4$ from both sides.
$2x$$=$$1$Simplify.
$\dfrac{2x}{\hl{2}}$$=$$\dfrac{1}{\hl{2}}$Divide both sides by $2$.
$x$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2}$Simplify.

Check: put $\frac{1}{2}$ in for $x$. $\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8} + \frac{4}{8} = \frac{5}{8}$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\dfrac{1}{8}x + \dfrac{1}{2}$$=$$\dfrac{1}{4}$The problem. The denominators are $8$, $2$ and $4$, so the LCD is $8$.
$\hl{8}\left(\dfrac{1}{8}x + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$$=$$\hl{8}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)$Multiply both sides by the LCD, $8$.
$x + 4$$=$$2$Distribute: $8 \cdot \frac{1}{8}x = x$, $8 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 4$ and $8 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 2$. No more fractions.
$x + 4 \hl{{} - 4}$$=$$2 \hl{{} - 4}$To undo adding $4$, subtract $4$ from both sides.
$x$$=$$-2$Simplify.

Check: put $-2$ in for $x$. $\frac{1}{8}(-2) + \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{4} + \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$.
