Problem 1

$6[4 - 2(7y - 1)]$$=$$8(13 - 8y)$The problem. Start with the innermost parentheses.
$6[4 - 14y + 2]$$=$$8(13 - 8y)$Distribute $-2$. Careful: $-2$ times $-1$ is $+2$.
$6[6 - 14y]$$=$$8(13 - 8y)$Combine like terms inside the brackets.
$36 - 84y$$=$$104 - 64y$Distribute the $6$ and the $8$.
$36 - 84y \hl{{} + 84y}$$=$$104 - 64y \hl{{} + 84y}$$-64$ is bigger than $-84$, so keep the $y$ terms on the right: add $84y$ to both sides.
$36$$=$$104 + 20y$Combine like terms.
$36 \hl{{} - 104}$$=$$104 + 20y \hl{{} - 104}$Subtract $104$ from both sides.
$-68$$=$$20y$Simplify.
$\dfrac{-68}{\hl{20}}$$=$$\dfrac{20y}{\hl{20}}$Divide both sides by $20$.
$-\dfrac{17}{5}$$=$$y$Simplify. $\frac{68}{20}$ reduces to $\frac{17}{5}$.

Check: put $-\frac{17}{5}$ in for $y$ in the original equation. Both sides come to $\frac{1608}{5}$, so $y = -\frac{17}{5}$ is the solution.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$12[1 - 5(4z - 1)]$$=$$3(24 + 11z)$The problem. Start with the innermost parentheses.
$12[1 - 20z + 5]$$=$$3(24 + 11z)$Distribute $-5$. Careful: $-5$ times $-1$ is $+5$.
$12[6 - 20z]$$=$$3(24 + 11z)$Combine like terms inside the brackets.
$72 - 240z$$=$$72 + 33z$Distribute the $12$ and the $3$.
$72 - 240z \hl{{} + 240z}$$=$$72 + 33z \hl{{} + 240z}$$33$ is bigger than $-240$, so keep the $z$ terms on the right: add $240z$ to both sides.
$72$$=$$72 + 273z$Combine like terms.
$72 \hl{{} - 72}$$=$$72 + 273z \hl{{} - 72}$Subtract $72$ from both sides.
$0$$=$$273z$Simplify.
$\dfrac{0}{\hl{273}}$$=$$\dfrac{273z}{\hl{273}}$Divide both sides by $273$.
$0$$=$$z$Zero divided by any nonzero number is $0$. An answer of $0$ is a real solution.

Check: put $0$ in for $z$. The left side is $12[1 - 5(-1)] = 12 \cdot 6 = 72$, and the right side is $3(24) = 72$.
