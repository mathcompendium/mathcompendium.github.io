Try It 2.11

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Solve: $\frac{1}{5}(n + 3) = \frac{1}{4}(n + 2)$.
  2. Solve: $\frac{1}{2}(m - 3) = \frac{1}{4}(m - 7)$.
Show a hint
Distribute first to remove the parentheses. Then multiply both sides by the LCD to clear the fractions.
Show the answers

  1. $n = 2$

  2. $m = -1$

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$\dfrac{1}{5}(n + 3)$$=$$\dfrac{1}{4}(n + 2)$The problem. Remove the parentheses first.
$\dfrac{1}{5}n + \dfrac{3}{5}$$=$$\dfrac{1}{4}n + \dfrac{1}{2}$Distribute on both sides.
$\hl{20}\left(\dfrac{1}{5}n + \dfrac{3}{5}\right)$$=$$\hl{20}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}n + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$The denominators are $5$, $4$ and $2$, so the LCD is $20$. Multiply both sides by $20$.
$4n + 12$$=$$5n + 10$Distribute: $20 \cdot \frac{1}{5} = 4$, $20 \cdot \frac{3}{5} = 12$, $20 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 5$, $20 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 10$.
$4n + 12 \hl{{} - 4n}$$=$$5n + 10 \hl{{} - 4n}$$5$ is bigger than $4$, so keep the $n$ terms on the right: subtract $4n$ from both sides.
$12$$=$$n + 10$Combine like terms.
$12 \hl{{} - 10}$$=$$n + 10 \hl{{} - 10}$To undo adding $10$, subtract $10$ from both sides.
$2$$=$$n$Simplify.

Check: put $2$ in for $n$. The left side is $\frac{1}{5}(5) = 1$, and the right side is $\frac{1}{4}(4) = 1$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\dfrac{1}{2}(m - 3)$$=$$\dfrac{1}{4}(m - 7)$The problem. Remove the parentheses first.
$\dfrac{1}{2}m - \dfrac{3}{2}$$=$$\dfrac{1}{4}m - \dfrac{7}{4}$Distribute on both sides.
$\hl{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}m - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)$$=$$\hl{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}m - \dfrac{7}{4}\right)$The LCD of $2$ and $4$ is $4$. Multiply both sides by $4$.
$2m - 6$$=$$m - 7$Distribute: $4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 2$, $4 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 6$, $4 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 1$, $4 \cdot \frac{7}{4} = 7$.
$2m - 6 \hl{{} - m}$$=$$m - 7 \hl{{} - m}$Keep the $m$ terms on the left: subtract $m$ from both sides.
$m - 6$$=$$-7$Combine like terms.
$m - 6 \hl{{} + 6}$$=$$-7 \hl{{} + 6}$To undo subtracting $6$, add $6$ to both sides.
$m$$=$$-1$Simplify.

Check: put $-1$ in for $m$. The left side is $\frac{1}{2}(-4) = -2$, and the right side is $\frac{1}{4}(-8) = -2$.
