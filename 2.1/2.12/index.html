Try It 2.12

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Solve: $\frac{3r + 5}{6} + 1 = \frac{4r + 3}{3}$.
  2. Solve: $\frac{2s + 3}{2} + 1 = \frac{3s + 2}{4}$.
Show a hint
Multiply both sides by the LCD. Put parentheses around each numerator first, since the fraction bar groups it.
Show the answers

  1. $r = 1$

  2. $s = -8$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$\dfrac{3r + 5}{6} + 1$$=$$\dfrac{4r + 3}{3}$The problem. The denominators are $6$ and $3$, so the LCD is $6$.
$\hl{6}\left(\dfrac{3r + 5}{6} + 1\right)$$=$$\hl{6}\left(\dfrac{4r + 3}{3}\right)$Multiply both sides by $6$.
$3r + 5 + 6$$=$$2(4r + 3)$The $6$s divide out on the left, and $6 \cdot 1 = 6$. On the right, $6 \div 3 = 2$. Keep the parentheses: the fraction bar groups the whole numerator.
$3r + 11$$=$$8r + 6$Combine like terms on the left, and distribute the $2$ on the right.
$3r + 11 \hl{{} - 3r}$$=$$8r + 6 \hl{{} - 3r}$$8$ is bigger than $3$, so keep the $r$ terms on the right: subtract $3r$ from both sides.
$11$$=$$5r + 6$Combine like terms.
$11 \hl{{} - 6}$$=$$5r + 6 \hl{{} - 6}$To undo adding $6$, subtract $6$ from both sides.
$5$$=$$5r$Simplify.
$\dfrac{5}{\hl{5}}$$=$$\dfrac{5r}{\hl{5}}$Divide both sides by $5$.
$1$$=$$r$Simplify.

Check: put $1$ in for $r$. The left side is $\frac{8}{6} + 1 = \frac{14}{6} = \frac{7}{3}$, and the right side is $\frac{7}{3}$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\dfrac{2s + 3}{2} + 1$$=$$\dfrac{3s + 2}{4}$The problem. The denominators are $2$ and $4$, so the LCD is $4$.
$\hl{4}\left(\dfrac{2s + 3}{2} + 1\right)$$=$$\hl{4}\left(\dfrac{3s + 2}{4}\right)$Multiply both sides by $4$.
$2(2s + 3) + 4$$=$$3s + 2$On the left, $4 \div 2 = 2$, and $4 \cdot 1 = 4$. On the right, the $4$s divide out.
$4s + 6 + 4$$=$$3s + 2$Distribute the $2$.
$4s + 10$$=$$3s + 2$Combine like terms.
$4s + 10 \hl{{} - 3s}$$=$$3s + 2 \hl{{} - 3s}$Keep the $s$ terms on the left: subtract $3s$ from both sides.
$s + 10$$=$$2$Combine like terms.
$s + 10 \hl{{} - 10}$$=$$2 \hl{{} - 10}$To undo adding $10$, subtract $10$ from both sides.
$s$$=$$-8$Simplify.

Check: put $-8$ in for $s$. The left side is $\frac{-13}{2} + 1 = -\frac{11}{2}$, and the right side is $\frac{-22}{4} = -\frac{11}{2}$.
