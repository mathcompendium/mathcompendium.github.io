Problem 1

$6(p - 3) - 7$$=$$5(4p + 3) - 12$The problem. There are parentheses on both sides.
$6p - 18 - 7$$=$$20p + 15 - 12$Distribute on both sides. The $-7$ and the $-12$ are outside the parentheses, so they just come down.
$6p - 25$$=$$20p + 3$Combine like terms.
$6p - 25 \hl{{} - 6p}$$=$$20p + 3 \hl{{} - 6p}$$20$ is bigger than $6$, so keep the $p$ terms on the right: subtract $6p$ from both sides.
$-25$$=$$14p + 3$Combine like terms.
$-25 \hl{{} - 3}$$=$$14p + 3 \hl{{} - 3}$To undo adding $3$, subtract $3$ from both sides.
$-28$$=$$14p$Simplify.
$\dfrac{-28}{\hl{14}}$$=$$\dfrac{14p}{\hl{14}}$Divide both sides by $14$.
$-2$$=$$p$Simplify.

Check: put $-2$ in for $p$. The left side is $6(-5) - 7 = -37$, and the right side is $5(-5) - 12 = -37$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$8(q + 1) - 5$$=$$3(2q - 4) - 1$The problem. There are parentheses on both sides.
$8q + 8 - 5$$=$$6q - 12 - 1$Distribute on both sides. The $-5$ and the $-1$ just come down.
$8q + 3$$=$$6q - 13$Combine like terms.
$8q + 3 \hl{{} - 6q}$$=$$6q - 13 \hl{{} - 6q}$$8$ is bigger than $6$, so keep the $q$ terms on the left: subtract $6q$ from both sides.
$2q + 3$$=$$-13$Combine like terms.
$2q + 3 \hl{{} - 3}$$=$$-13 \hl{{} - 3}$To undo adding $3$, subtract $3$ from both sides.
$2q$$=$$-16$Simplify.
$\dfrac{2q}{\hl{2}}$$=$$\dfrac{-16}{\hl{2}}$Divide both sides by $2$.
$q$$=$$-8$Simplify.

Check: put $-8$ in for $q$. The left side is $8(-7) - 5 = -61$, and the right side is $3(-20) - 1 = -61$.
