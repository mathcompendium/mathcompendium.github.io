The signs are different, so the product is negative. Multiply as if they were whole numbers, then count the decimal places in both factors.

  1. $-27.4815$

    $45 \cdot 6107 = 274{,}815$, and there are $1 + 3 = 4$ decimal places.

  2. $-87.6148$

    $1079 \cdot 812 = 876{,}148$, and there are $2 + 2 = 4$ decimal places.

Problem 1

$-4.5(6.107)$The signs are different, so the product is negative.
Multiply as if they were whole numbers: $45 \cdot 6107 = 274{,}815$.
$4.5$ has one decimal place and $6.107$ has three, so the product has $1 + 3 = 4$.
$=$$-27.4815$Count four places from the right for the point, and make it negative.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$-10.79(8.12)$The signs are different, so the product is negative.
Multiply as if they were whole numbers: $1079 \cdot 812 = 876{,}148$.
Each factor has two decimal places, so the product has $2 + 2 = 4$.
$=$$-87.6148$Count four places from the right, and make it negative.
