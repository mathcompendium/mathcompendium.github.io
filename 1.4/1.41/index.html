Problem 1

$\sqrt{36}$$=$$6$ⓐ $6^2 = 36$.
$\sqrt{169}$$=$$13$ⓑ $13^2 = 169$.
$-\sqrt{225}$$=$$-15$ⓒ $15^2 = 225$. Careful: the negative sign is in front of the radical, not under it, so it stays.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$\sqrt{16}$$=$$4$ⓐ $4^2 = 16$.
$\sqrt{196}$$=$$14$ⓑ $14^2 = 196$.
$-\sqrt{100}$$=$$-10$ⓒ $10^2 = 100$, and the negative sign in front stays.
