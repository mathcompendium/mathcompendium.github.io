Multiply a Decimal by Powers of Ten

Try It 1.37

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Multiply $2.58$ by ⓐ $10$ ⓑ $100$ ⓒ $1000$.
  2. Multiply $14.2$ by ⓐ $10$ ⓑ $100$ ⓒ $1000$.
Show a hint
Move the decimal point to the right one place for each zero in the power of ten, writing zeros as placeholders when you run out of digits.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $25.8$ ⓑ $258$ ⓒ $2{,}580$

  2. ⓐ $142$ ⓑ $1{,}420$ ⓒ $14{,}200$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$2.58 \cdot 10$$=$$25.8$ⓐ $10$ has one zero, so move the point one place to the right.
$2.58 \cdot 100$$=$$258$ⓑ $100$ has two zeros: two places to the right.
$2.58 \cdot 1000$$=$$2{,}580$ⓒ Three places, but there are only two digits after the point, so write a zero as a placeholder.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$14.2 \cdot 10$$=$$142$ⓐ One zero: move the point one place to the right.
$14.2 \cdot 100$$=$$1{,}420$ⓑ Two places, with one placeholder zero.
$14.2 \cdot 1000$$=$$14{,}200$ⓒ Three places, with two placeholder zeros.
