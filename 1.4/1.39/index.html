Show a hint
The place value of the last digit gives the denominator: $0.234$ is $\dfrac{234}{1000}$; then simplify. For a fraction, divide the numerator by the denominator.
Show the answers

  1. ⓐ $\dfrac{117}{500}$ ⓑ $-0.875$

  2. ⓐ $\dfrac{3}{125}$ ⓑ $-0.375$

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

ⓐ $0.234$ as a fraction
$0.234$The last digit, $4$, is in the thousandths place.
$=$$\dfrac{234}{1000}$So the denominator is $1000$.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{2} \cdot 117}{\cancel{2} \cdot 500}$Both are even: divide out a $2$.
$=$$\dfrac{117}{500}$$117 = 3 \cdot 3 \cdot 13$ and $500 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 5$ share no factor, so this is simplified.
ⓑ $-\dfrac{7}{8}$ as a decimal
$-\dfrac{7}{8}$The fraction bar means divide: $7 \div 8$.
$8$ doesn't go into $7$, so write $7.000$. $8$ goes into $70$ eight times (remainder $6$), into $60$ seven times (remainder $4$), and into $40$ five times exactly.
$=$$-0.875$The fraction is negative, so the decimal is too.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

ⓐ $0.024$ as a fraction
$0.024$The last digit, $4$, is in the thousandths place.
$=$$\dfrac{24}{1000}$So the denominator is $1000$.
$=$$\dfrac{\cancel{8} \cdot 3}{\cancel{8} \cdot 125}$$24 = 8 \cdot 3$ and $1000 = 8 \cdot 125$. Divide out the $8$.
$=$$\dfrac{3}{125}$
ⓑ $-\dfrac{3}{8}$ as a decimal
$-\dfrac{3}{8}$The fraction bar means divide: $3 \div 8$.
Write $3.000$. $8$ goes into $30$ three times (remainder $6$), into $60$ seven times (remainder $4$), and into $40$ five times exactly.
$=$$-0.375$The fraction is negative, so the decimal is too.
