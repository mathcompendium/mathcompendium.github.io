Try It 1.38

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Divide: $-23.492 \div (-0.04)$.
  2. Divide: $-4.11 \div (-0.12)$.
Show a hint
Same signs, so the quotient is positive. Move both decimal points until the divisor is a whole number, then divide.
Show the answers

  1. $587.3$

    Move both points two places: $2349.2 \div 4 = 587.3$.

  2. $34.25$

    Move both points two places: $411 \div 12 = 34.25$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$-23.492 \div (-0.04)$The signs are the same, so the quotient is positive.
$=$$23.492 \div 0.04$
$=$$2349.2 \div 4$Move both decimal points two places to the right, so the divisor is a whole number.
$=$$587.3$Divide. The point in the quotient sits right above the point in the dividend.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$-4.11 \div (-0.12)$The signs are the same, so the quotient is positive.
$=$$4.11 \div 0.12$
$=$$411 \div 12$Move both decimal points two places to the right.
$=$$34.25$Divide, writing zeros after the point when you run out of digits to bring down.
