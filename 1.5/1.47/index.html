Problem 1

$-27a + (-48a) + 27a$The problem. $-27a$ and $27a$ are opposites.
$=$$-27a + 27a + (-48a)$Use the Commutative Property to put the opposites side by side.
$=$$0 + (-48a)$A number plus its opposite is $0$.
$=$$-48a$Adding $0$ changes nothing.

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.
