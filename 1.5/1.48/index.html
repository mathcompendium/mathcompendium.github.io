Problem 1

$\dfrac{9}{16} \cdot \dfrac{5}{49} \cdot \dfrac{16}{9}$The problem. $\frac{9}{16}$ and $\frac{16}{9}$ are reciprocals.
$=$$\dfrac{9}{16} \cdot \dfrac{16}{9} \cdot \dfrac{5}{49}$Use the Commutative Property to put the reciprocals side by side.
$=$$1 \cdot \dfrac{5}{49}$A number times its reciprocal is $1$.
$=$$\dfrac{5}{49}$Multiplying by $1$ changes nothing.
