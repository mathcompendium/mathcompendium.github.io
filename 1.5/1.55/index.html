Distribute first, carrying the minus sign with the number in front of the parentheses. Then combine like terms.
Show the answers

  1. $3 - 3x$

    $9 - 3x - 6 = 3 - 3x$.

  2. $2x - 20$

    $7x - 5x - 20 = 2x - 20$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$9 - 3(x + 2)$The problem. Careful: you can't do $9 - 3$ first, because the $3$ is multiplied by the parentheses.
$=$$9 - 3 \cdot x - 3 \cdot 2$Distribute the $3$, keeping the minus in front.
$=$$9 - 3x - 6$$3 \cdot x = 3x$ and $3 \cdot 2 = 6$.
$=$$3 - 3x$Combine the constants: $9 - 6 = 3$.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$7x - 5(x + 4)$The problem. Multiplication comes before subtraction, so distribute first.
$=$$7x - 5 \cdot x - 5 \cdot 4$Distribute the $5$, keeping the minus in front.
$=$$7x - 5x - 20$$5 \cdot x = 5x$ and $5 \cdot 4 = 20$.
$=$$2x - 20$Combine like terms: $7x - 5x = 2x$.
