Problem 1

$23r + 14s + 9r + 15s$The problem. $23r$ and $9r$ are like terms, and so are $14s$ and $15s$.
$=$$23r + 9r + 14s + 15s$Use the Commutative Property to put the like terms next to each other.
$=$$32r + 29s$Add the coefficients.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$37m + 21n + 4m - 15n$The problem. $37m$ and $4m$ are like terms, and so are $21n$ and $-15n$.
$=$$37m + 4m + 21n - 15n$Use the Commutative Property to put the like terms together. The minus sign moves with the $15n$.
$=$$41m + 6n$Combine: $37 + 4 = 41$ and $21 - 15 = 6$.
