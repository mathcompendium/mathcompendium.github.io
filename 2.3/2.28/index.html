Try It 2.28

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Use the formula $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ to solve for $b$.
  2. Use the formula $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ to solve for $h$.
Show a hint
Multiply both sides by $2$ to remove the fraction, then divide by the other letter.
Show the answers

  1. $b = \dfrac{2A}{h}$

  2. $h = \dfrac{2A}{b}$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$b$ is multiplied by two things: $\frac{1}{2}$ and $h$. Undo each multiplication.
$A$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2}bh$The formula.
$\hl{2} \cdot A$$=$$\hl{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}bh$To remove the fraction, multiply both sides by $2$.
$2A$$=$$bh$Simplify: $2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1$.
$\dfrac{2A}{\hl{h}}$$=$$\dfrac{bh}{\hl{h}}$Divide both sides by $h$.
$\dfrac{2A}{h}$$=$$b$Simplify.
$b$$=$$\dfrac{2A}{h}$Write $b$ on the left.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$h$ is multiplied by two things: $\frac{1}{2}$ and $b$. Undo each multiplication.
$A$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2}bh$The formula.
$\hl{2} \cdot A$$=$$\hl{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}bh$To remove the fraction, multiply both sides by $2$.
$2A$$=$$bh$Simplify: $2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 1$.
$\dfrac{2A}{\hl{b}}$$=$$\dfrac{bh}{\hl{b}}$Divide both sides by $b$.
$\dfrac{2A}{b}$$=$$h$Simplify.
$h$$=$$\dfrac{2A}{b}$Write $h$ on the left.
