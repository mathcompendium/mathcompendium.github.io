Try It 2.38

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. John puts the base of a $13$-foot ladder five feet from the wall of his house. How far up the wall does the ladder reach?
  2. Randy wants to attach a $17$-foot string of lights to the top of the $15$-foot mast of his sailboat. How far from the base of the mast should he attach the end of the light string?
Show a hint
Find the right triangle: the slanted piece is the hypotenuse. Substitute into $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, isolate the square, and take the positive square root.
Show the answers

  1. The ladder reaches $12$ feet.

  2. He should attach the lights $8$ feet from the base of the mast.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

The wall and the ground meet at a right angle, so the ladder makes a right triangle. The ladder is the hypotenuse, $13$ feet; the ground is a leg, $5$ feet. Let $x$ = how far up the wall the ladder reaches.
$a^2 + b^2$$=$$c^2$The Pythagorean Theorem.
$5^2 + x^2$$=$$13^2$Substitute the legs, $5$ and $x$, and the hypotenuse, $13$.
$25 + x^2$$=$$169$Simplify the squares.
$25 \hl{{} - 25} + x^2$$=$$169 \hl{{} - 25}$To isolate the variable, subtract $25$ from both sides.
$x^2$$=$$144$Simplify.
$x$$=$$\sqrt{144}$Use the definition of square root. $x$ is a length, so it's positive.
$x$$=$$12$Simplify.
The ladder reaches $12$ feet up the wall.

Check: $5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169$, which is $13^2$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

The mast stands at a right angle to the deck, so the light string makes a right triangle. The string is the hypotenuse, $17$ feet; the mast is a leg, $15$ feet. Let $x$ = the distance from the base of the mast.
$a^2 + b^2$$=$$c^2$The Pythagorean Theorem.
$15^2 + x^2$$=$$17^2$Substitute the legs, $15$ and $x$, and the hypotenuse, $17$.
$225 + x^2$$=$$289$Simplify the squares.
$225 \hl{{} - 225} + x^2$$=$$289 \hl{{} - 225}$To isolate the variable, subtract $225$ from both sides.
$x^2$$=$$64$Simplify.
$x$$=$$\sqrt{64}$Use the definition of square root. $x$ is a length, so it's positive.
$x$$=$$8$Simplify.
He should attach the lights $8$ feet from the base of the mast.

Check: $15^2 + 8^2 = 225 + 64 = 289$, which is $17^2$.
