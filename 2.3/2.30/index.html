Try It 2.30

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. Solve the formula $A = P + Prt$ for $t$.
  2. Solve the formula $A = P + Prt$ for $r$.
Show a hint
Subtract $P$ from both sides to get the term with the letter alone, then divide by the other letters it is multiplied by.
Show the answers

  1. $t = \dfrac{A - P}{Pr}$

  2. $r = \dfrac{A - P}{Pt}$

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

$t$ is multiplied by $Pr$, then $P$ is added. Undo them in reverse order: the adding first, then the multiplying.
$A$$=$$P + Prt$The formula. $t$ is in the last term on the right.
$A \hl{{} - P}$$=$$P + Prt \hl{{} - P}$To undo adding $P$, subtract $P$ from both sides.
$A - P$$=$$Prt$Simplify: $P$ and $-P$ add to zero.
$\dfrac{A - P}{\hl{Pr}}$$=$$\dfrac{Prt}{\hl{Pr}}$To undo multiplying by $Pr$, divide both sides by $Pr$.
$\dfrac{A - P}{Pr}$$=$$t$Simplify. Careful: the whole difference is divided by $Pr$.
$t$$=$$\dfrac{A - P}{Pr}$Write $t$ on the left.
Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

$r$ is multiplied by $Pt$, then $P$ is added. Undo the adding first, then the multiplying.
$A$$=$$P + Prt$The formula. $r$ is in the last term on the right.
$A \hl{{} - P}$$=$$P + Prt \hl{{} - P}$Subtract $P$ from both sides.
$A - P$$=$$Prt$Simplify: $P$ and $-P$ add to zero.
$\dfrac{A - P}{\hl{Pt}}$$=$$\dfrac{Prt}{\hl{Pt}}$Divide both sides by $Pt$.
$\dfrac{A - P}{Pt}$$=$$r$Simplify. Careful: the whole difference is divided by $Pt$.
$r$$=$$\dfrac{A - P}{Pt}$Write $r$ on the left.
