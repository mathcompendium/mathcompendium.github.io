Find the Length and Width of a Rectangle from Its Perimeter

Try It 2.35

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. The length of a rectangle is seven more than twice the width. The perimeter is $110$ inches. Find the length and width.
  2. The width of a rectangle is eight yards less than twice the length. The perimeter is $86$ yards. Find the length and width.
Show a hint
Let the variable stand for the side the other one is described by. Write the other side in terms of it, substitute into $P = 2L + 2W$, and solve.
Show the answers

  1. The length is $39$ inches and the width is $16$ inches.

  2. The length is $17$ yards and the width is $26$ yards.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

The length is described in terms of the width, so let $W$ = the width. Then $2W + 7$ = the length.
$P$$=$$2L + 2W$The perimeter of a rectangle.
$110$$=$$2(2W + 7) + 2W$Substitute. The length goes in parentheses, since all of it is multiplied by $2$.
$110$$=$$4W + 14 + 2W$Distribute the $2$.
$110$$=$$6W + 14$Combine like terms.
$110 \hl{{} - 14}$$=$$6W + 14 \hl{{} - 14}$Subtract $14$ from both sides.
$96$$=$$6W$Simplify.
$\dfrac{96}{\hl{6}}$$=$$\dfrac{6W}{\hl{6}}$Divide both sides by $6$.
$16$$=$$W$Simplify. That's the width.
For the length, $2W + 7 = 2 \cdot 16 + 7 = 39$.
The length is $39$ inches and the width is $16$ inches.

Check: $2 \cdot 39 + 2 \cdot 16 = 78 + 32 = 110$, the perimeter.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Here the width is described in terms of the length, so let $L$ = the length. Then $2L - 8$ = the width.
$P$$=$$2L + 2W$The perimeter of a rectangle.
$86$$=$$2L + 2(2L - 8)$Substitute. The width goes in parentheses, since all of it is multiplied by $2$.
$86$$=$$2L + 4L - 16$Distribute the $2$. Careful: $2 \cdot (-8) = -16$.
$86$$=$$6L - 16$Combine like terms.
$86 \hl{{} + 16}$$=$$6L - 16 \hl{{} + 16}$Add $16$ to both sides.
$102$$=$$6L$Simplify.
$\dfrac{102}{\hl{6}}$$=$$\dfrac{6L}{\hl{6}}$Divide both sides by $6$.
$17$$=$$L$Simplify. That's the length.
For the width, $2L - 8 = 2 \cdot 17 - 8 = 26$.
The length is $17$ yards and the width is $26$ yards.

Check: $2 \cdot 17 + 2 \cdot 26 = 34 + 52 = 86$, the perimeter.
