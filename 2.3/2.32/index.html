Find the Height of a Triangle from Its Area

Try It 2.32

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. The area of a triangular church window is $90$ square meters. The base of the window is $15$ meters. What is the window’s height?
  2. A triangular tent door has an area of $15$ square feet. The height is five feet. What is the length of the base?
Show a hint
Write $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$, substitute the two numbers you know, multiply them, then divide to find the third.
Show the answers

  1. The window’s height is $12$ meters.

  2. The length of the base is $6$ feet.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

We know the area, $90$ square meters, and the base, $15$ meters. Let $h$ = the height.
$A$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2}bh$Write the formula for the area of a triangle.
$90$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 15 \cdot h$Substitute the area and the base.
$90$$=$$7.5h$Multiply on the right: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 15 = 7.5$.
$\dfrac{90}{\hl{7.5}}$$=$$\dfrac{7.5h}{\hl{7.5}}$Divide both sides by $7.5$.
$12$$=$$h$Simplify.
The window’s height is $12$ meters.

Check: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 15 \cdot 12 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 180 = 90$, the area of the window.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

We know the area, $15$ square feet, and the height, $5$ feet. Let $b$ = the length of the base.
$A$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2}bh$Write the formula for the area of a triangle.
$15$$=$$\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot b \cdot 5$Substitute the area and the height.
$15$$=$$2.5b$Multiply on the right: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 = 2.5$.
$\dfrac{15}{\hl{2.5}}$$=$$\dfrac{2.5b}{\hl{2.5}}$Divide both sides by $2.5$.
$6$$=$$b$Simplify.
The length of the base is $6$ feet.

Check: $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 5 = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 30 = 15$, the area of the door.
