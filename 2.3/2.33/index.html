Find the Angles of a Right Triangle

Try It 2.33

Your turn: problems like the one in the video
  1. The measure of one angle of a right triangle is $50$ more than the measure of the smallest angle. Find the measures of all three angles.
  2. The measure of one angle of a right triangle is $30$ more than the measure of the smallest angle. Find the measures of all three angles.
Show a hint
Let $a$ be the smallest angle. The right angle is $90^\circ$, and the three angles add up to $180^\circ$.
Show the answers

  1. The measures of the angles are $20^\circ$, $70^\circ$, and $90^\circ$.

  2. The measures of the angles are $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $90^\circ$.

Show the worked solution

The same steps as the video. Each ▶ button plays the part of the video where that step is explained.

Problem 1

Let $a$ = the smallest angle. The second angle is $50$ more, $a + 50$, and the third is the right angle, $90^\circ$.
The measures of the angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$.
$a + (a + 50) + 90$$=$$180$Substitute the three angles.
$2a + 140$$=$$180$Combine like terms: $a + a = 2a$ and $50 + 90 = 140$.
$2a + 140 \hl{{} - 140}$$=$$180 \hl{{} - 140}$Subtract $140$ from both sides.
$2a$$=$$40$Simplify.
$\dfrac{2a}{\hl{2}}$$=$$\dfrac{40}{\hl{2}}$Divide both sides by $2$.
$a$$=$$20$Simplify. That's the smallest angle.
The second angle is $a + 50 = 20 + 50 = 70^\circ$, and the third is the right angle, $90^\circ$.
The measures of the angles are $20^\circ$, $70^\circ$, and $90^\circ$.

Check: $20 + 70 + 90 = 180$.

Problem 2

Try problem 2 again on your own first, using problem 1 as your guide. Then compare.

Let $a$ = the smallest angle. The second angle is $30$ more, $a + 30$, and the third is the right angle, $90^\circ$.
The measures of the angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$.
$a + (a + 30) + 90$$=$$180$Substitute the three angles.
$2a + 120$$=$$180$Combine like terms: $a + a = 2a$ and $30 + 90 = 120$.
$2a + 120 \hl{{} - 120}$$=$$180 \hl{{} - 120}$Subtract $120$ from both sides.
$2a$$=$$60$Simplify.
$\dfrac{2a}{\hl{2}}$$=$$\dfrac{60}{\hl{2}}$Divide both sides by $2$.
$a$$=$$30$Simplify. That's the smallest angle.
The second angle is $a + 30 = 30 + 30 = 60^\circ$, and the third is the right angle, $90^\circ$.
The measures of the angles are $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $90^\circ$.

Check: $30 + 60 + 90 = 180$.
